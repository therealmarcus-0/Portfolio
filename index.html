About Me

Your Photo

I'm Marcus, a student at St. Mark's School. I'm interested in computer science, app development, and building things that people actually use.

I started coding because I wanted to make stuff, not just learn about it. My biggest project right now is a school app for St. Mark's that I'm building from scratch and preparing to beta test with real users. I also do freelance web development for local businesses and individuals.

Outside of tech, I play golf, stay active with fitness training, and I've been teaching myself the basics of personal finance and investing — partly because I think it's important, and partly because I want to build tools around it eventually.

I'm planning to study computer science in college and I'm aiming for top STEM programs. But what motivates me most right now is making useful things and getting better at my craft every day.
